# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Developers managing the school management dashboard. They require tools tailored for a developer context.

## Product Purpose
Manage and overview day-to-day operations and high-level health of a school, tailored specifically for a developer-centric workflow.

## Brand Personality
Minimal, focused, high-density (like a dev tool).

## Anti-references
Avoid bloated, slow UI. No marketing fluff. Purely functional and utilitarian.

## Design Principles
- High information density over spacious layouts.
- Keyboard and power-user friendly.
- Fast, instant feedback without superfluous animations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
High contrast / Dark mode primary.
